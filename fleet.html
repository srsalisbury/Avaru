Where it began

AERES

Our first ship, built by the team that became Avaru.

Paradox Design and Uncle Andy Industries built the Aeres to race, and every choice on it came down to speed. The engineers who worked on it got hooked. When the project ended, twenty-odd of them quit their jobs so they could keep building race ships. That became Avaru.
