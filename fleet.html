Where it began

AERES

Our first ship, built by the team that became Avaru.

Paradox Design and Uncle Andy Industries built the Aeres to race, and every choice on it came down to speed. The engineers who worked on it got hooked. When the project ended, twenty-odd of them quit their jobs so they could keep building race ships. That became Avaru.

Atmospheric Sprinter · Our first official ship

EKA

Finnish for “the start.”

Built by twenty-odd engineers with almost no money, for the Astra Racing League's first Atmospheric Sprinter race. It isn't aerodynamically perfect, and it isn't built to do everything. It's built to run the same blazing lap, again and again, without a sound.
