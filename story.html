Our story

From the Aeres to the .

How twenty-odd engineers walked away from good jobs to build what they love.

01

The Aeres

When Paradox Design and Uncle Andy Industries agreed to build the Aeres together, each company sent some of its best engineers to a joint team. Neither side expected much from the arrangement beyond a good ship.

They got one. The Aeres did better than anyone had planned for. But the bigger surprise was the team itself. Engineers from two very different shops had learned to work as one: they argued well, finished each other's sketches, and by the end of the project they were already talking about what they would build next.

02

Separate ways

There was no next. Both companies decided their futures lay apart, and the joint team was disbanded. Its engineers went back to their old desks and their old assignments.

They kept in touch anyway. Late messages, rough sketches and half-finished ideas passed back and forth: ships nobody had asked for and nobody was going to build. Until one evening someone asked the question that had been sitting under all of it:

Why don't we make our own company, and build what we love instead of what we're told?

03

The walkout

Twenty-odd engineers from the original team said yes. In the summer of 2026, they all resigned, and Avaru Racing was born.

Their first plan was a smuggler-class ship, big and capable. It did not go to plan. Components for a hull that size were hard to find and harder to afford, and the few sponsors willing to back an unknown team had only so much to give. For a while, it looked like Avaru would end before it had started.

04

A new class

Then the Astra Racing League held its first race for a new class: the Atmospheric Sprinter. The ships were smaller and faster. Just as important, they were far cheaper to build.

The engineers at Avaru saw the opening at once. The smuggler build went back on the shelf, and every hand turned to a sprinter.

05

Building the

They designed it, built it, tested it and refined it, then did all of it again. They named their first official ship for what it was: .

It was not aerodynamically perfect, the way Paradox ships were. It was not supremely versatile, the way UAI ships had been. It was something new. It wasn't flashy, and it didn't call attention to itself.

06

First race

On race day, the waited in the pitlane while its crew worked on it in silence. Then it took to the skies, and not a single fan, pilot or sponsor could help but stare. For an instant, the colors of the aurora borealis flashed across its hull. Lap after lap it ran: silent, consistent, blazing fast.

In that moment, everyone watching saw what the engineers had seen years before. This new age, this league, was at the mercy of the Avaru . Racing would never be the same again.
